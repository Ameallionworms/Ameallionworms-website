Bulk Storage

Best practices for storing your 10,000-count bags.

Temperature

Store bags in a refrigerator between 42°F and 50°F to safely induce dormancy. Do not freeze.

Keep it Dry

Never add wet vegetables or water to refrigerated bags. Condensation causes fatal mold.

Shelf Life

For maximum vitality with zero maintenance, sell or use the inventory within 4 to 6 weeks.

Note on Transferring: If moving worms from our bulk bags into retail display cups, always ensure the new containers are breathable and well-ventilated.

Frass Guide

Organic insect fertilizer for stronger, healthier plants.

Frass is a premium organic insect manure. It provides slow-release nutrients and is packed with chitin. When plants detect chitin, it triggers their natural defenses, resulting in stronger root systems and thicker cell walls.

How to Apply Frass

1 Soil Mixing (For New Plants)

Mix 1 cup of frass per 1 cubic foot of potting soil before planting to establish a long-term nutrient base.

2 Top Dressing (For Established Plants)

Sprinkle a thin layer over the topsoil, gently scratch it into the surface, and water thoroughly. Reapply every 3 to 4 weeks.

3 Liquid Tea

Steep 1/2 cup of frass in 1 gallon of dechlorinated water for 2 to 24 hours. Use the strained liquid as a root drench or foliar spray.
